ABSTRACT 
 
             Road accidents have become a major global concern, with a significant 
number of fatalities occurring due to the negligence of traffic safety measures. 
Among these, the failure of two -wheeler riders to wear helmets is one of the 
leading causes of severe hea d injuries and deaths. Traditional methods of 
monitoring such violations rely heavily on manual observation, which is 
inefficient, time -consuming, and prone to human error. Therefore, there is a 
growing need for an automated system that can ensure effectiv e monitoring and 
enforcement of traffic rules. 
 
This project presents an AI -based traffic surveillance system that focuses on 
detecting motorcyclists who are not wearing helmets. The system utilizes 
advanced computer vision techniques to analyze video input captured from CCTV 
cameras or recorded traffic footage. OpenCV is used for processing video frames, 
enabling the system to extract and analyze images in real time. This forms the 
foundation for further object detection and violation identification. 
 
A YOLO (You Only Look Once) based deep learning model is employed to detect 
motorcycles and identify whether the rider is wearing a helmet. YOLO is known 
for its high speed and accuracy in real -time object detection, making it suitable 
for traffic monitori ng applications. When a helmet violation is detected, the 
system highlights the rider and captures the corresponding frame, ensuring that 
the violation is properly recorded for further processing. 
 
To identify the violating vehicle, the system extracts the number plate region from 
the captured frame. The processed image is then passed to Tesseract OCR, which 
extracts the vehicle registration number. This automated number plate recognition 
helps in accurately identifying violators without manual intervention.

CHAPTER 1 
INTRODUCTION 
Road safety has become a critical issue worldwide due to the rapid increase 
in the number of vehicles and traffic density. Among various types of road users, 
two-wheeler riders are more vulnerable to accidents and injuries. One of the 
primary reasons for s evere injuries and fatalities is the failure to wear helmets 
while riding. Despite strict traffic rules and awareness campaigns, many riders 
neglect helmet usage, leading to serious consequences. Ensuring compliance with 
helmet laws through manual monitori ng is difficult, especially in busy urban 
environments. 
 
Traditional traffic monitoring systems rely heavily on human intervention, 
where traffic police or surveillance personnel manually observe violations through 
direct monitoring or CCTV footage. This approach is not only time -consuming 
but also prone to huma n error, fatigue, and inefficiency. In high -traffic areas, it 
becomes almost impossible to monitor every vehicle continuously. As a result, 
many violations go unnoticed, reducing the effectiveness of traffic law 
enforcement and compromising road safety. 
 
This project proposes an AI -based traffic surveillance system specifically 
designed to detect motorcyclists who are not wearing helmets. The system 
processes video input using OpenCV and applies a YOLO-based model to detect 
motorcycles and helmet usage. When a violation is identified, the system captures 
the frame and extracts the vehicle number plate using OCR techniques. The 
detected information is then displayed on a dashboard for monitoring. By focusing 
on helmet detection for two-wheelers, the system aims to enhance road safety and 
provide a practical solution for modern traffic management.

2.1 Helmet Detection 
 
Helmet detection has become an important research area in traffic safety 
systems, especially for two -wheeler riders. Early approaches relied on traditional 
image processing techniques such as edge detection, color segmentation, and shape 
analysis to identify helmets. However, these methods were limited in accuracy and 
struggled in complex environments with varying lighting conditions and 
occlusions. 
 
With the advancement of deep learning, modern approaches use object detection 
models such as YOLO (You Only Look Once) to improve performance. YOLO -
based models are capable of detecting motorcycles and identifying helmet usage in 
real time with high accuracy. Several studies have demonstrated that deep learning 
models outperform traditional methods by providing faster processing and better 
adaptability to real -world scenarios. This makes them highly suitable for traffic 
surveillance systems. 
 
     Recent research focuses on optimizing helmet detection models for real -time 
applications using CCTV footage. These systems are designed to handle multiple 
vehicles simultaneously and detect violations efficiently. The integration of deep 
learning technique s has significantly improved detection accuracy and reduced 
false positives, making automated helmet detection a reliable solution for traffic 
monitoring.

2.2 Number Plate Recognition: 
 
         Number plate recognition is a crucial component of automated traffic 
surveillance systems, as it enables identification of violating vehicles. Earlier 
methods used basic image processing techniques such as contour detection and 
template matching to locate number plates. However, these methods were not 
robust enough to handle variations in lighting, angle, and image quality. .Modern 
systems use a combination of deep learning models and Optical Character 
Recognition (OCR) for improved accuracy. Object  detection models are used to 
locate the number plate region, while OCR tools like Tesseract are used to extract 
text from the image. Preprocessing techniques such as grayscale conversion, noise 
reduction, and thresholding are applied to enhance the image before text extraction, 
improving recognition accuracy. 
 Recent studies have shown that integrating deep learning with OCR provides 
efficient and reliable number plate recognition in real -time applications. These 
systems can accurately detect and read vehicle registration numbers from video 
feeds, even in challenging conditions. This makes them an essential component of 
intelligent traffic management systems and supports automated enforcement of 
traffic rules. 
Recent advancements in number plate recognition systems have focused on 
improving accuracy under challenging real-world conditions such as low lighting, 
motion blur, and varying camera angles. Deep learning -based approaches, 
particularly those combining co nvolutional neural networks with OCR, have 
shown significant improvements in detecting and recognizing number plates from 
dynamic video streams. These systems are capable of handling different plate 
formats and fonts, making them more adaptable to diverse environments. As a 
result, modern number plate recognition techniques play a vital role in automated 
traffic enforcement by ensuring reliable identification of vehicles involved in 
violations.
